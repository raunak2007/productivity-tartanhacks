How It Works

Cognitive Science-Based Optimization

Utilio uses advanced AI trained on cognitive science principles to analyze your tasks and minimize the hidden costs of context switching.

Context Switching Costs

High Cost (15-25 mins): Switching between deep work and administrative tasks

Medium Cost (10-15 mins): Transitioning between completely different contexts

Low Cost (2-5 mins): Moving between similar tasks

The Solution

By batching similar tasks together and optimizing the order, you can maintain cognitive flow, reduce mental fatigue, and save hours every week.
