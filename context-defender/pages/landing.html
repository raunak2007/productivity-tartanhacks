About Utilio

Every time you switch between different types of tasks—from coding to email, writing to meetings—your brain pays a steep price. It can take up to 23 minutes to fully regain focus after an interruption.

Research shows that multitasking reduces productivity by up to 40%. The constant task-switching drains mental energy, causes errors, and leaves you exhausted by the end of the day.

Utilio solves this by intelligently batching similar tasks together. Instead of scattering your attention, it creates blocks of focused work time. The result? Deeper concentration, faster completion, and significantly less cognitive fatigue.

Built during TartanHacks 2026, Utilio transforms how knowledge workers approach their day—defending against the invisible costs of chaotic scheduling.
